FleetPulse — QA Fill Sheet (English)

Step 1 — Basic Info
Project Name: FleetPulse
Industry: saas-b2b
Current Stage: mvp
One-line Description: Real-time dispatch optimization for mid-sized logistics fleets to cut empty miles, fuel costs, and late deliveries.

Step 2 — Value Proposition
1.1. Main customer problem: Mid-sized logistics operators lose margin due to inefficient dispatching: manual route planning, poor visibility into vehicle status, last-minute order changes, and empty backhauls. Dispatch teams spend hours re-planning daily, drivers waste time waiting, and service levels drop when ETAs are wrong.
1.2. Your solution: FleetPulse connects to telematics + TMS/ERP data and recommends optimized daily dispatch plans in minutes. It continuously re-optimizes routes based on real-time traffic, vehicle location, driver hours, and new orders, and provides ETA updates + exception alerts to dispatchers and customers.
1.3. Uniqueness of your solution: Unlike static route planners, FleetPulse is built for dynamic dispatch (same-day changes) and uses live telematics + constraints (HOS, capacity, dock time windows). We provide explainable recommendations (why a stop was moved) and a backhaul-matching module that proactively suggests profitable return loads.
1.4. Key advantages over competitors: Dynamic re-optimization; backhaul recommendation engine; fast integrations; dispatcher-friendly UI; customer ETA portal.
1.5. Measurable value for customer: Reduce empty miles by 8-15% and fuel spend by 5-10%. Save 1-2 dispatcher hours per day per depot. Improve on-time delivery by 3-7 pp and reduce detention/late fees. Typical ROI target: pay back subscription in under 3 months for fleets of 30-150 vehicles.
